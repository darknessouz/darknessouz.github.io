The idea

Balance it before your players do

Every game economy hides questions. How long until a player can afford the next upgrade? Does gold pile up faster than it is spent? How often does the hero win? Kretslopp lets you answer them by drawing the economy and running it, instead of guessing.

It uses the resource-flow diagrams many game designers already know from Joris Dormans’ work: pools, sources, drains, converters, gates and more. Draw it, press Play, change a number and press Play again.

What it can do

Draw it, play it, forecast it

Draw your economy

  • Pools, sources, drains, converters, gates, registers, delays and end conditions.
  • Connect them with flows, triggers and conditions, and drag everything around.
  • Every number can be a formula: dice like 2D6+1, chances or the name of a pool.

Watch it play

  • Step through it or let it run, with moving resources and a live chart.
  • Click the interactive parts while it runs, just like a player would.
  • See at a glance how much came in and how much went out.

Forecast hundreds of plays

  • The median, the average and the range most plays land in, step by step.
  • How often each ending happens, and at which step: your time to goal.
  • Give a button a rule like “Hero below 10”, and the simulated player presses it.

Catches mistakes

  • Marks parts that can never do anything, and resources that get stuck.
  • Points out names in formulas that do not exist.
  • Click a warning to jump straight to the problem.

Your diagrams stay yours

  • No account. Your work is saved on your own device as you go.
  • Keep as many diagrams as you like, and export them as files to share or back up.
  • Four examples to start from: income, crafting, upgrades and a battle.

Comfortable to use

  • Light and dark mode, in English or Swedish.
  • Change every keyboard shortcut to suit you.
  • Built-in help with the notation, the formulas and the shortcuts.
