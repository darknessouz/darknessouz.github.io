CoinfolkIdle Coin Clicker

Pip lives inside your coin! A cozy idle clicker with a real story. Tap your coin, watch it grow from copper to bronze, silver, gold and beyond, and travel with Pip through sixteen hand-drawn worlds.

  • Free to play

The story

The Baron wants every coin grey

Baron Tarnish wants every coin in every world dull and grey, so he has hired the meanest folks around to guard them: an Angry Goose, a pirate captain, a bridge troll, a swamp witch, a volcano dragon and many more.

Beat them, open the portal and follow the Baron all the way to his grey city. Pip will be with you the whole way. He has a face for everything: he grins, frets, naps and gets dizzy, and he cheers you on when a fight goes well.

What is inside

Busy or relaxing, you choose

A story you take part in

  • Walk Pip from stop to stop on a living map and meet the folk who live there.
  • Choose what Pip says. The characters remember, and the map shows it.
  • Every talk has jokes, small dramas and a voice of its own for everyone you meet.

Bosses with tricks

  • 48 bosses, three in every world, each with a trick of their own.
  • Break a shield, follow a runaway minecart, tap along with a bell.
  • Train Pip, wait for the right moment and watch a tough boss go down.

A world that feels lived in

  • Sixteen hand-drawn worlds, each with its own music and secret.
  • Build Coinbury house by house, through rain, snow, storms and starry nights.
  • Play three small games in town for extra prizes.

Collect and grow

  • Ten machines keep earning while you are away. One tap collects it all.
  • Catch 86 kinds of critters from Nordic, Slavic and Alpine folk tales.
  • Meet Pip’s eight friends, dress them for every world and hunt 92 achievements.

Play your way

  • Tap fast for big combos, or sit back and let the machines work.
  • Pop in for a minute, or stay for an hour.
  • Turn the effects down if you like it calm. Older phones get a lighter mode by themselves.

Fair and free

  • Everything can be earned for free.
  • Ads are optional and only play when you choose to watch one.
  • No internet and no account needed. A save code brings your game to a new phone.
