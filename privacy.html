Coinfolk

Privacy policy.

Last updated 9 November 2026

Coinfolk is an idle coin game made by an independent developer. This page explains what information the game uses and why. If you have questions, email shamesauce@hotmail.com.

Your game progress

Your coins, upgrades and settings are saved only on your own device. Coinfolk has no accounts, and the developer does not receive or store this information. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

In Settings → Save backup you can copy a save code that holds your whole game, to keep it safe or move it to another device. The code stays with you: the game never sends it anywhere. Anyone you give the code to can load your game with it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you when your vault is full or when today's gift is waiting. The reminders are created on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app can show a short ad when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are never shown without you asking for one. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location based on the IP address, device information, diagnostic information about how the app performs, and how you use the app and the ads. The ad service starts when the app starts, so this can happen even if you never watch an ad.

If you are in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Read more in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. Google Play tells the app which items you have bought so they can be delivered and restored, for example after reinstalling.

The web version

The web version of Coinfolk shows no ads and sells nothing. Your progress is saved in your browser only.

The website, including this page, is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. Read more in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Coinfolk is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
